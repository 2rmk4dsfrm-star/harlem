FAQ

Self-employed. Paid weekly. Mon–Sat routes.

Can't see yours? Ask us on WhatsApp

Am I employed or self-employed?

Self-employed. You're paid weekly for the parcels you deliver, and you're responsible for your own tax and National Insurance through Self Assessment.

How much will I earn?

Most routes pay 80p per parcel, and Harrogate and York pay £1.00. On 80p routes, daily pay is usually between £75 and £200 depending on how many parcels you deliver. Try the pay calculator.

When do I get paid?

Weekly. We'll confirm your pay day when you join.

What vehicle do I need?

A car or a short-wheelbase (SWB) van. If you have a long-wheelbase (LWB) van, message us about other routes.

What is hire & reward insurance?

It's insurance that covers you to carry goods for payment, sometimes sold as courier insurance. Standard social, domestic and commuting cover won't protect you while you deliver parcels, so you need it in place before you start.

Where do routes start?

Every route starts at our Leeds depot. We'll send you the address and your start time once your application packs are complete.

Which days will I work?

Routes are permanent and run Monday to Saturday. Tell us which days you're looking for when you message.

Do I need parcel delivery experience?

It's preferred, but not essential. Tell us about any delivery or driving work you've done when you message.

How quickly can I start?

Often the same week. Once your packs are checked and your insurance is in place, you can collect your first route.
